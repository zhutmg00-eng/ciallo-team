Amorepacific

2026 Honoree in Beauty Tech

Beauty Tech

Developed in collaboration with MIT, SkinsightTM is an advanced electronic skin platform for precise skin aging analytics. The system features an integrated sensor patch, Bluetooth module, and a mobile app including AI. With a high-sensitivity strain sensor, SkinsightTM measures micron-level skin deformations, improving prediction accuracy. The patch’s sensors monitor UV/blue light, temperature, moisture, and mechanical deformation; auxetic kirigami and dumbbell hole patterns ensure reliable long-wear data. Real-time Bluetooth transmission enables continuous 24-hour monitoring. Using each user’s representative 24-hour lifestyle profile, AI identifies which factors are dominant in that individual’s skin aging and models the composite exposome (overlap and acceleration effects among the four exposures). Leveraging 24-hour signals plus our longitudinal dataset, the system predicts the location and degree of future aging, then recommends targeted products and routines matched to the user’s dominant drivers.
