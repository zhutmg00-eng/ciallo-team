# Accuracy and clinical relevance of an automated, algorithm-based analysis of facial signs from selfie images of women in the United States of various ages, ancestries and phototypes

- **ID / 序号**: 1.6
- **类别**: 03_学术文献 / 学术文献_主题1
- **URL**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10087370/
- **网页标题**: Accuracy and clinical relevance of an automated, algorithm‐based analysis of facial signs from selfie images of women in the United States of various ages, ancestries and phototypes: A cross‐sectional observational study - PMC
- **作者/机构**: Flament F 等
- **期刊/年份**: JEADV (2023)

## 资料包原生注释
- **核心结论/摘要**: 1,041 名女性、50 名皮肤科医生参与：5 项体征 r≥0.75，毛孔 r=0.63，色素类体征相关最弱（r=0.40），在最深肤色人群中尤其弱。
- **赛题应用价值**: 直接证明「色素／肤色测量」在不同肤色之间有偏差，应写进方案的风险与公平性部分。
